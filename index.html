One platform, every pharmacy in the loop.

MedOrd connects medicine agencies and medical stores — stock, orders, billing and delivery, tracked in one place instead of scattered across calls and paper ledgers.

See what it does Download status

The problem

Ordering between agencies and medical stores is still mostly manual — phone calls, paper ledgers, scattered spreadsheets. Stock visibility is poor and invoicing is slow, with no reliable way to track an order end to end.

The solution

Agencies publish live stock and offers, stores order directly against it, and every order is tracked from placement through OTP-verified delivery — billing and reporting handled automatically along the way.

Built for two very different days

An agency's day is stock, billing and dispatch. A store's day is ordering, tracking and getting paid quickly. MedOrd gives each side the view built for their work.
